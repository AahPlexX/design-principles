Craft

The reference tells you why a rule holds. Craft is where you prove you can apply it — short, hands-on courses, each paired with one principle page, each built around real practice instead of more reading.

Each lesson is short: a quick framing, then one practice question with real feedback on the spot. Nothing to sign up for — your progress is saved right in this browser.
