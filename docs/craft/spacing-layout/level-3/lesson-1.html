Level 3, Lesson 1: One consistent left edge per column

A landing page centers every headline, paragraph, and button down the middle of the page. It looks tidy in a single screenshot. Scrolling through the whole thing is a different story.

A page centers every heading, paragraph, and button instead of aligning them to a shared left edge. What problem does this create for someone scanning down the page?

This is the "centering everything" mistake: center alignment has no consistent edge for the eye to follow down a page, while left-aligned (in LTR languages) text and controls give the reader one vertical line to scan. It's why the checklist calls for one clear left edge per column of content — alignment, not just spacing, is part of showing a reader what's related.
