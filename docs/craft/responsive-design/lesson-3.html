Lesson 3: Reprioritizing content instead of hiding it

Space runs out fast on a narrow screen, and deleting something is often the easiest way to make room. But "delete" and "rearrange" produce very different results for the person holding the phone — one keeps them from finishing what they came to do, the other just asks them to scroll a little further to reach it.

A restaurant's mobile layout is cramped, so the redesign removes the phone number and address on small screens, keeping them only in the desktop layout. Is this a good responsive design decision?

Hiding content on mobile instead of re-prioritizing it often removes something a mobile user specifically needed — like a phone number or address someone is looking up on their phone precisely because they're about to call or visit. The fix is to re-arrange the layout so it still fits, not to cut the content because the screen is narrow.
