Lesson 2: Giving lines room to breathe

Line length controls how far the eye travels sideways. Line height controls how far it travels down to find the next line — and it's just as easy to get wrong in the opposite direction, by squeezing lines together instead of spacing them too far apart.

A paragraph of several long sentences is set with line-height: 1.1. What happens to the reading experience?

Line height under 1.4 makes lines of text feel like they're touching, which slows reading, especially for longer paragraphs. Body text needs a line height of roughly 1.4–1.6× its font size to give the eye room to land on the correct line.
