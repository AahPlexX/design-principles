Level 2, Lesson 2: The 3:1 minimum for large text, and the exact size/weight cutoffs

The 4.5:1 minimum is for body text. Large text — 24px and up, or 18.66px and up if it's bold — only needs to clear 3:1. Bigger letterforms are inherently easier to read at lower contrast, so the bar genuinely is lower, not just relaxed as a shortcut.

A 28px bold heading measures 3.2:1 against its background. Does it pass?

At 28px bold, this qualifies as "large text" under the size/weight thresholds (24px+ regular, or 18.66px+ bold), so the 3:1 minimum applies instead of 4.5:1. 3.2:1 clears that bar, even though it would fail the stricter body-text minimum.
