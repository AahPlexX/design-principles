Level 2, Lesson 1: The 4.5:1 minimum for body text

Contrast isn't a feeling — it's a number. Body text needs a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against its background. Any real tool (browser DevTools, a contrast checker) gives you this number directly; you never have to eyeball it.

Body text is #1a1a2e on a #ffffff background. A contrast checker says the ratio is 7.7:1. Does this pass for body text?

The minimum for body text is 4.5:1. 7.7:1 is well above that — comfortably readable even on a dim screen or in bright light, which is exactly the margin the minimum is meant to guarantee.
