Lesson 2: Confirmshaming vs. a neutral no

Every dialog with an accept and a decline button is making the same request twice, once in each button's label. Read the decline button on its own, separate from the offer next to it, and the tone gives the pattern away.

A discount modal offers two buttons: "Yes, upgrade me!" and "No thanks, I like paying more than I have to." What makes the decline button a dark pattern?

This is confirmshaming: wording a decline option to guilt the reader ("No thanks, I don't want to save money") instead of a neutral "No thanks" turns a simple choice into an emotional one. The fix is neutral wording on both buttons, not removing the decline option.
