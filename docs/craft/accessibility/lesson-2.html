Lesson 2: Labels vs. placeholders

Placeholder text looks like a label sitting inside the field, but it isn't one. It disappears the moment someone starts typing, and some screen readers don't announce it at all. A form field needs a real, persistently-visible label connected to it.

A signup form has <input type="email" placeholder="Email address"> with no <label> anywhere near it. What's missing?

A <label for="email">Email address</label> tied to the input's id stays visible the whole time someone's filling out the field, and is what a screen reader actually announces as the field's name — a placeholder is a hint, not a substitute for it.

Full explanation: Accessibility.
